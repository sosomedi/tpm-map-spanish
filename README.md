# TPM Awarded Plants Map 🗺️

An interactive web-based map displaying plants and companies awarded with Total Productive Maintenance (TPM) worldwide.

> **Language Notice:** The map interface, tooltips, and country names are provided in **Spanish** (`tpm-map-spanish`).

---

## ⚠️ Status & Data Disclaimers

This project is a **work in progress** and subject to continuous updates and refinements. Please keep the following in mind:

* **Language & Interface:** The map and filter controls are rendered in Spanish.
* **Japanese Company Names:** Entries for Japanese plants have been directly romanized (phonetically transliterated from Japanese) rather than using their official English corporate names or trademarked international brand names. These entries will be standardized in upcoming revisions.
* **Ongoing Updates:** Company lists and yearly mappings are subject to minor corrections as historical records are audited.
